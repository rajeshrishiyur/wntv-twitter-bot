Situation/Task + Action + Results — 220 words

SITUATION/TASK

At Elementum, 2017 to 2019, a six-site manufacturer learned about component shortages when the line stopped. Eight SAP data domains never met, and the contract manufacturer's bill of materials arrived as an unreconciled feed. I owned the definitions and the data-integrity program; the client's SAP teams and our engineers built feeds. The target was a shortage visible a lead time early.

ACTION

I wrote the definition before any screen: grain SKU by location by date; supply is on-hand plus production plan plus released planned orders only; I refused to count unfirmed orders. Every domain went live behind a published data-quality gate its owner could fail, inventory and production first. Feeds ran as a nightly full load to re-baseline and heal a missed change, plus intraday deltas for receipts and shipments. A row that failed validation went to an exception queue with a source owner and was fixed at the source, never in our warehouse. Each morning landed numbers were reconciled to the SAP lists before the alert was trusted. Nobody was mandated off their spreadsheet.

RESULTS

Class-A forecast accuracy went from 70 to 85 percent. The shortage alert was live in three months, firing when days of cover fell inside the lane's lead time. Planner effort fell by half; two million dollars of safety stock came out under a rule Finance signed.

IF THEY PROBE

What did a quality gate look like? Per-domain completeness at or above the bar, reconciliation passing daily, false alarms trending down. Below the gate the feed got fixed, not automated.

Who could fail a wave? The domain owner. The business could say no to its own data, which is why they trusted it when they said yes.

The contract manufacturer's data? Phantom assemblies found by math, exploding bills of materials against their MRP; revision overlaps held in an effective-date quarantine with a human disposition, never auto-loaded. Six sites in three waves over five months, zero auto-loaded overlaps.

Is the two million annual? No. One-time working capital, released only where forecast error and lead-time error had both improved. I never add it to the lane program's million and a half.

What slipped? The part-number mapping worklist gated longer than planned; it went to the sponsor as a re-sequenced date with the coverage number attached.
