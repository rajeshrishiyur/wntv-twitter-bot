Answer strategy — business first, systems as enablers

  • Use the JD's four outcome words as the results frame: reliability (did the promise hold?), responsiveness (how much earlier did we see it?), cost efficiency ($ with its basis label), asset utilization (capital, labor-hours, capacity released).
  • Action = operating-model redesign in gated phases — Discovery → Plan & Design → Build & Test → Cutover → Hypercare, each spoken as entry bar · what I delivered · exit bar. Say the gate that fired, not the meeting that happened.
  • Every dollar carries its basis word in the same breath — record · user anchor · modeled · priced. A record dollar is said flat; a modeled dollar is said with its inputs. Never promote a modeled number to record.
  • Program-lead voice. I owned definitions, rules, gates and the signature chain; engineers, SIs and vendor teams configured and built to the spec I signed. The only platform personally configured in canon is Manhattan DOM.
  • The pitch ends on the primary metric. Then stop. Silence after the pitch is the interviewer filling their STAR worksheet; their probes are the second half of the answer.
