A2. What “dual inventory” precisely means here

Definition, grounded in p. 3–4 and rows 1–6, 19–21, 24, 35–36

One physical building, one inventory location, two selling identities, channel reserves. Before: UDC ran as two systemic warehouses — Dotcom (inventory location 801, selling location 800) and Retail (901) — with physically separate storage and systemic transfers between them. After: a single DC inventory location 901 holds both channels' stock comingled in shared storage locations; Retail keeps 901 as its selling identity and Dotcom gets a new selling location 900 mapped onto 901; purchase orders are generated and received once against 901 with three reserve buckets — Dotcom Reserve, Retail Reserve, Uncommitted — maintained by the comingle reserve logic replicated 100% from Canada. 801/800 are decommissioned with no conversion of historical transactions; during the interim, Frame auto-copies on-hand, on-order and the Dotcom split % from 801 to 900 until decommission, and the May conversion script moves 100% of inventory on 05/01 with lost/found included, zeroes 801 in the Yantriks publish and republishes 901/900 in full.

So “dual” means dual-channel, not duplicate. It is one pool with a channel partition, not two copies of inventory. The sentence to say: “Two channels, one pool: one inventory location, two selling identities, and the reserve logic decides who may promise what.”
