Two probes

How did you know it was routing and not the application?

By decomposition before remediation: the static/dynamic split put ~5s of the China time on assets the app server never touched, and the same assets served in ~500ms from the US — the code was constant, the path was not. The domain's missing geo-DNS / reverse proxy and the double CDN explained both halves of the gap without a single application change.

Why not just stand up a China region?

A region is a re-architecture with a compliance tail; the evidence said the bottleneck was routing and CDN topology, which fixes in weeks. The sequenced plan — WAF, double-CDN removal, regional CDN, tuning — bought most of the win at a fraction of the risk, and the smoke-test protocol meant each step was reversible.
